Dr. Maxat Kassen's book - "Understanding Systems of e-Government: e-Federalism and e-Centralism in the United States and Kazakhstan" (Rowman & Littlefield: New York, 2015)

About the book:

The book introduces readers to the results of comparative cross-institutional analysis on e-governance policies in the United States and Kazakhstan. The results of field studies and ethnographic research that Dr. Maxat Kassen conducted in the USA as a Fulbright Scholar in 2011-2012 and consequent trips provided a rich empirical basis to write the monograph.
